AI pa Nos Hobenan

Un kurso di shete siman di inteligensia artifisial na Papiamentu — grátis pa e partisipantenan, pa 20 hóben di Kòrsou. Kada un ta sali ku algu ku e mes a traha i ku e por usa, i ku un sertifikado konhunto di Ministerio di VVRP i BMC. A seven-week artificial-intelligence course in Papiamentu — free for the participants, for 20 young people in Curaçao. Everyone leaves with something they built themselves and can actually use, plus a joint certificate from the Ministry of VVRP and BMC.

Presentá na: Ministerio di VVRPPresented to: Ministry of VVRP Pa: Bakhuis Management & ConsultancyBy: Bakhuis Management & Consultancy Ougùstùs 2026August 2026

Basá riba fundeshi internashonal. E kurso ta basá riba e UNESCO AI Competency Framework for Students (2024) i riba e struktura di Elements of AI (Universidat di Helsinki) — no riba un programa inventá. Built on international foundations. The course is based on the UNESCO AI Competency Framework for Students (2024) and on the structure of Elements of AI (University of Helsinki) — not on an invented programme.

UNESCO

AI Competency Framework for Students (2024)

12 kompetensia den 4 dimenshon (mentalidat sentrá riba hende · étika di AI · téknika i aplikashon · diseño di sistema), na 3 nivel: Komprendé → Apliká → Krea. Kada módulo di nos kurso ta mará na un dimenshon i un nivel. 12 competencies across 4 dimensions (human-centred mindset · ethics of AI · AI techniques and applications · AI system design), at 3 levels: Understand → Apply → Create. Every module of our course is tied to one dimension and one level.

Helsinki

Elements of AI

E kurso di AI grátis di mas konosí na mundu — Universidat di Helsinki, 2018, mas ku 2 mion studiante den 170 pais, na 26 idioma. The best-known free AI course in the world — University of Helsinki, 2018, more than 2 million students across 170 countries, in 26 languages.

Loke nan ta trahaWhat they make

Kada kursista ta kaba e kurso ku algu ku e por usa Every participant finishes the course with something they can use

Nos no ta pidi nan traha un aplikashon. Na siman 2 nan ta skohe for di un lista di ehèmpelnan konkreto i nan ta adaptá esaki na nan mes situashon. E regla ta simpel: mester ta un kos ku bo lo usa di bèrdè otro luna. We are not asking them to build an app. In week 2 they choose from a list of concrete examples and adapt it to their own situation. The rule is simple: it has to be something you will genuinely use next month.

1

Un plan di estudioA study plan

Pa yud'é ku su estudionan di skol.To help them with their schoolwork.

2

Un CV i karta di solisitutA CV and cover letter

Pa yud'é hanja trabou.To help them find a job.

3

Un plan di redes sosialA social-media plan

Pa un negoshi di famia of pa e kos ku e ta bende banda.For a family business or for whatever they sell on the side.

4

Un karta ofisial kontestáAn official letter, answered

Un karta di bèrdè — di banko, SVB, skol, seguro of hür — komprondí i kontestá.A real letter — from the bank, SVB, school, insurer or landlord — understood and answered.

5

Preparashon pa un entrevistaInterview preparation

Praktiká e entrevista, i sali ku e preguntanan ku e mester hasi.Practise the interview, and leave with the questions they should ask.

6

Un tarea ku ta ripitíA repeating task

Un kos ku e ta hasi tur siman i ku AI por hasi mas lihé.Something they do every week that AI can make faster.

Ken ku ke, por traha na pareha. Dos hende, un kos, i tur dos ta presentá. Asina ningun hende ta keda pegá pasobra e no sa kon kuminsá su so. Anyone who wants to can work in pairs. Two people, one piece of work, and both present. That way nobody gets stuck because they don't know how to start alone.

Ko-diseñoCo-design

E partisipantenan ta yuda forma e kursoThe participants help shape the course

Nos ta diseñá e kurso asina ku nos ta tene kuenta ku loke ta e nesesidat di e kursistanan. Nos ta siña e kursistanan e kosnan básiko di AI, pero tambe nos ta wak ki nesesidat tin i nos ta adaptá e kurso a base di esaki. We design the course around what the participants actually need. We teach them the basics of AI, but we also look at what they need and adapt the course to that.

Un luna proméOne month before

Formulario di inskripshonRegistration form

Tres pregunta: kiko bo ta studia of traha, pa kiko bo ke usa AI, i kiko bo ke sa hasi na fin di e kurso. E kontestanan ta yega na tempu pa adaptá e material. Three questions: what you study or do, what you want to use AI for, and what you want to be able to do by the end. The answers arrive in time to adapt the material.

Siman 1Week 1

Último 20 minütLast 20 minutes

Awor ku nan a mira kiko AI ta hasi di bèrdè, nan por bisa mihó kiko nan ke. Nos ta ahustá e simannan ku ta bini. Now that they have seen what AI actually does, they can say better what they want. We adjust the coming weeks.

Siman 2Week 2

Nan ta skohe nan kosThey choose their thing

Kada un ta skohe for di e lista i ta adaptá esaki na su situashon. For di e momento ei, tur les ta sirbi e kos ei. Each one picks from the list and adapts it to their own situation. From that moment on, every lesson serves that.

E programaThe programme

Shete siman, dos ora kada simanSeven weeks, two hours a week

Siman 1Week 1 Kiko AI ta — i kiko e no taWhat AI is — and what it is not
Promé kombersashon ku un chatbot, riba nan mes telefòn. UNESCO: mentalidat sentrá riba hende · Komprendé. First conversation with a chatbot, on their own phone. UNESCO: human-centred mindset · Understand.
Siman 2Week 2 Kon pidi bon — i skohe bo kosHow to ask well — and pick your thing
Skirbi instrukshon kla. Kada un ta skohe for di e lista. UNESCO: téknika i aplikashon · Apliká. Writing clear instructions. Each participant picks from the list. UNESCO: techniques and applications · Apply.
Siman 3Week 3 Informashon ku bo por konfiaInformation you can trust
Ki ora AI ta inventá kos, i kon bo ta chèk esaki. UNESCO: étika di AI · Komprendé. When AI makes things up, and how to check it. UNESCO: ethics of AI · Understand.
Siman 4Week 4 Bo datos i bo privasidatYour data and your privacy
Kiko bo no ta pega den un chat, i pakiko. UNESCO: étika di AI · Apliká. What you never paste into a chat, and why. UNESCO: ethics of AI · Apply.
Siman 5Week 5 Traha riba bo kosBuild your thing
Klas di traha. Kada un riba su propio kos, ku yudansa. UNESCO: Krea. Working session. Everyone on their own piece, with support. UNESCO: Create.
Siman 6Week 6 Mehorá i praktikáImprove and rehearse
Feedback di otro partisipantenan, i preparashon di e presentashon. UNESCO: Krea. Feedback from the other participants, and preparing the presentation. UNESCO: Create.
Siman 7Week 7 Presentashon i seremoniaPresentation and ceremony
Kada un ta mustra loke el a traha, i ta risibí su sertifikado. Each participant shows what they made and receives their certificate.
+ Seshon èkstra — opshonalExtra session — optional
Pa ken ku ke sigui ku e sertifikado ofisial di Google AI Essentials: un seshon di preparashon. For anyone who wants to go on to the official Google AI Essentials certificate: one preparation session.
